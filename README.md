🚀 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

I recently developed an interactive Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard focused on analyzing operational efficiency, profitability, logistics performance, and product-level business insights.

📌 What I Observed:
• Shipping costs varied significantly across transportation modes.
• Certain product categories contributed much higher revenue than others.
• Supplier profitability was uneven across the supply chain.
• Defect rates and manufacturing costs directly impacted overall profitability.
• Revenue trends fluctuated over the years, highlighting operational inconsistencies.

📌 What I Did:
• Cleaned and transformed raw supply chain data using Power Query.
• Created KPI measures for Revenue, Profit, Products Sold, Shipping Cost, Manufacturing Cost, and Defect Rate using DAX.
• Generated a 10-year time-series structure for trend analysis.
• Designed interactive visualizations for:
  - Revenue trend analysis
  - Product performance tracking
  - Supplier profitability analysis
  - Transportation cost analysis
  - Regional revenue distribution
• Built an enterprise-style dashboard UI with slicers and business-focused reporting.

📌 Impact & Insights:
• Enabled quick identification of high-performing product categories and suppliers.
• Improved visibility into transportation cost distribution and operational expenses.
• Helped uncover areas with higher defect rates and inventory-related risks.
• Simplified complex operational data into an interactive dashboard for faster business decision-making.
• Demonstrated how business intelligence tools can improve supply chain monitoring and strategic planning.

🛠 Tools & Technologies:
